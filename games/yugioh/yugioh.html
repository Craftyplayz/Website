Yugioh Deck Builder Guide

Go to https://www.ygoprog.com/PackOpener and make an account to save your card list

First select 12 of the first pack (Legend of Blue Eyes) then open them all

Next to "Save to Binder" click "New" and name it whatever you want then click Create

Then click "Save" to save your card list

At the top of the page go to the tab labeled "Binders"

Click Select Binder and select the binder that you just made

Click Export and then click Export as banlist

Put the downloaded file into the ProjectIgnis\lflists Folder

Restart EDOPro then go to Decks

Click Ban List and select the banlist you just made

Select Limit and set it to "Allowed"

Now make your deck from the list of cards avaliable

Start your first Duel


The winner of the duel get to pick a card from the losers list of cards and add it to their deck (even if the loser wasnt playing that card)

Winner

In the binders section under "All Cards" search for the name of the card you picked

Click the + to add the card to your deck

Loser

In the binders section under "Your Binders" search for the card the winner chose and click the - to remove that card from your deck
