Elden Ring

Overview

Elden Ring is a 2022 action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki, with worldbuilding by George R. R. Martin, it is a single-player open-world game with multiplayer elements, set in the Lands Between. It received universal acclaim and won over 300 Game of the Year awards.

Key facts

  • Release: February 25, 2022 (PS4, PS5, Windows, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S); Nintendo Switch 2 version, Elden Ring Tarnished Edition, August 28, 2026
  • Developer: FromSoftware
  • Publisher: Bandai Namco Entertainment
  • Director: Hidetaka Miyazaki (co-director Yui Tanimura; producer Yuzo Kojima)
  • Platforms: PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch 2
  • Genre: Action role-playing, open world
  • Series: Standalone title (spin-off: Elden Ring Nightreign)

Gameplay

Played in third person, the game is an open-world exploration of the Lands Between, with six main areas and a steed named Torrent. Combat covers melee and ranged weapons, spells, Ashes of War, stealth and crafting. Sites of grace act as checkpoints, and runes serve as currency for leveling up and purchases. Co-op and player-versus-player multiplayer are included.

Story/Setting

The Lands Between are ruled by demigods after the Elden Ring was shattered. The player is a Tarnished exile who sets out to repair the Ring and become the Elden Lord. There are six possible endings, depending on player choices.

Development

Production began in early 2017, after the Dark Souls III DLC The Ringed City, and ran alongside Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice. The game was revealed at E3 2019. A closed beta was held in November 2021, and the launch was delayed from January 21 to February 25, 2022. Launch performance issues, especially on PC, were addressed through patches.

Reception

  • Metacritic: PC 94, PS5 96, Xbox Series X/S 96, Switch 2 90
  • OpenCritic: 98% of critics recommend it
  • Over 300 Game of the Year awards, including The Game Awards 2022 and the D.I.C.E. Awards 2023
  • Sales: 13.4 million copies by the end of March 2022; 30 million shipped by April 2025; the fastest-selling Bandai Namco game
  • Shadow of the Erdtree sold over 10 million copies

Legacy

The expansion Shadow of the Erdtree, set in the Land of Shadow, was released on June 20, 2024. The multiplayer-focused spin-off Elden Ring Nightreign followed in 2025. A film adaptation was announced in May 2025, produced by A24 and Bandai Namco and directed by Alex Garland, slated for March 3, 2028.
